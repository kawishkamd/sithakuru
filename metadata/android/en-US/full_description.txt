Sithakuru is a Free and Open-Source Sinhalese keyboard.

## Keyboard Layouts
- English
- Wijesekara
- Singlish

## Other facilities
- Light or Dark colors
- Show/hide key borders
- Show Sinhala letters on keys in Wijesekara layout
- Change keyboard height and font size
- Erase or move cursor with swiping

## Privacy
- No internet permission: nothing you type leaves your device
- Optional clipboard history is stored on the device only, as plain text in the app's private storage
- Clips that apps mark as sensitive (such as copied passwords) and anything copied in incognito fields are never saved
- Clipboard history can be turned off, auto-deleted after an hour or a day, or cleared at any time
