Sithakuru is a Free and Open-Source Sinhalese keyboard.

## Keyboard Layouts
- English
- Wijesekara
- Singlish

## Other facilities
- Light or Dark colors
- Show/hide key borders
- Show Sinhala letters on keys in Wijesekara layout
- Change keyboard height and font size
- Erase or move cursor with swiping

## Privacy
- No internet permission: nothing you type leaves your device
- Clipboard history is on by default. It is stored on the device only, as plain text in the app's private storage
- Not saved: clips an app marks as sensitive (Android 13 and newer; for example, passwords from many password managers), and anything on the clipboard while you type in a password or incognito field
- Clipboard history can be turned off (this deletes what is saved), auto-deleted after an hour or a day (the default), or cleared at any time
